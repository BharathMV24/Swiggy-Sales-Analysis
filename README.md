# 🍔 Swiggy Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes Swiggy food delivery sales data using Python in Jupyter Notebook.  
The dashboard provides business insights related to sales, customer ratings, food categories, city performance, and order trends.

The goal of this project is to perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and create visualizations to understand customer behavior and business performance.

---

# 📊 Business Requirement Document (BRD)

## KPI's

### 1. Total Sales (₹)
Overall revenue generated from food orders.

### 2. Average Rating
Customer satisfaction level across all restaurants.

### 3. Average Order Value (₹)
Average revenue generated per order.

### 4. Ratings Count
Total number of customer reviews received.

### 5. Total Orders
Total number of food orders.

---

# 📈 Charts & Visualizations

## 1. Monthly Sales Trend
Shows how total sales fluctuate month by month.

## 2. Daily Sales Trend
Highlights revenue and order variations across weekdays.

## 3. Total Sales by Food Type (Veg vs Non-Veg)
Compares revenue contribution between Veg and Non-Veg food categories.

## 4. Total Sales by State (Map Visualization)
Displays state-wise revenue distribution.

## 5. Quarterly Performance Summary
Combines Sales, Ratings, and Orders by quarter.

## 6. Top 5 Cities by Sales
Identifies cities contributing the highest revenue.
